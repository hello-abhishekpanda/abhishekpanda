ARCHITECT MINDSET / BEFORE THE FRAMEWORK

Five decisions behind every design.

Architecture is the disciplined management of trade-offs. Use these lenses before selecting a pattern, protocol, database, cloud service, or AI model.

05DECISION LENSES
01

Boundaries

Who owns behavior and data? Where can change happen independently?

02

Contracts

What can callers rely on? How does the contract evolve without breaking them?

03

Failure

What times out, retries, duplicates, degrades, or needs compensation?

04

Evidence

Which logs, metrics, traces, tests, and SLOs prove the system works?

05

Evolution

What is the simplest reversible choice for today and the next change?
